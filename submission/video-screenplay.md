# Demo video screenplay

Target under 3:00. Left column is what gets recorded, right is the voiceover. About 330 spoken words.

## Prep, not recorded

- Julia logged in, in one window. Fresh signup page ready in another.
- Dry-run the agent prompts once so responses are warm. Record waits in full, cut them in the edit.
- Record inside ChatGPT's browser (or Chrome with WebMCP enabled) for scenes 4 to 6, chat panel visible beside the page so tool calls are on screen.
- Password stays masked.

## Scene 1, 0:00 to 0:18. Julia's September, month view. Slow scroll.

> I spent the summer building my startup and planning my freshman year with the same models, and the model that built my entire backend was a useless machine on Stanford Axess, forgetting everything about me in a matter of seconds. The problem was context and browsing, so I built Acorn, the academic planning workspace for Stanford that you and your agent work on together.

## Scene 2, 0:18 to 0:45. Julia flythrough. Week view 4s, Academics 3s, Activities 3s, Scratchpad 3s, Activity panel 3s.

> This is Julia, a sample of a fully built workspace. Her agent picked her sections around ballet rehearsals and the Friday work-study shift she keeps protected, her clubs put their meetings on her calendar, her quick notes sit next to the goal her agent structured into milestones, and the activity log shows which changes came from her and which came from the agent.

## Scene 3, 0:45 to 1:05. From scratch. Signup, then onboarding: Name "Srivatsav Kannan", University Stanford, Entered 2026, Graduating 2030, Enter my workspace. Hold on the empty calendar.

> Now let me make mine from scratch. Onboarding is my name and three timeline facts, and the workspace starts out empty.

## Scene 4, 1:05 to 1:50. The braindump. Type to the agent:

"I'm Srivatsav, an incoming freshman. I'm into AI, healthcare, and startups. I want a research assistant spot by spring, and I lift in the mornings, so no classes before 10. Put all of that in my workspace, and mark CS 106B and MATH 51 as courses I'm interested in for autumn."

Flip once to Scratchpad and Academics while tools fire.

> Instead of filling out forms, I tell my agent who I am. The page registers twenty-two tools with the browser using WebMCP, which means the agent is writing to the same workspace my own clicks write to instead of screen-reading and guessing. By the time it finishes, my goal, my morning rule, and the courses I named are all sitting in the workspace.

## Scene 5, 1:50 to 2:10. By hand. Activities, Add your own: a club with a weekly Thursday 18:00 meeting. Show the calendar. Then ask:

"What does my Thursday look like now?"

> It works in the other direction too. I add a club myself with its Thursday meeting, it shows up on the calendar, and when I ask my agent about my Thursday it already knows, because we are reading and writing the same context.

## Scene 6, 2:10 to 2:40. The schedule. Ask:

"Build my autumn schedule around all of that. Real sections only."

Let the tools fire, then hold on the filled week view.

> Then I ask it to build my autumn. The catalog underneath is Stanford's real one, 15,618 courses with sections that pair each lecture with its discussion, and the agent works through the real combinations, keeps my mornings free, and puts the result on my calendar. The same model that built my backend planned my quarter just as well.

## Scene 7, 2:40 to 2:52. Landing page, cursor still.

> Acorn is live at acornplan.vercel.app. Next I want to ship it barebones and let the agent build the catalog for any school, because whatever the two of you put down, you come back and it is still there.
